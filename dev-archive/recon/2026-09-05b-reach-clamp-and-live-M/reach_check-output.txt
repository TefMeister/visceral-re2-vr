what the clamp ignores:
  the clavicle segment is 0.1264 m and is NOT counted, so the measured reach is a
  LOWER bound on true reach - the shoulder girdle can carry the pivot further out.
  Combined with x0.98 the clamp is conservative twice over: it can fire on a target
  the real arm could have held. clamp= in the 1 Hz summary is what settles whether it does.

bound on run 12 (the flat runs already verified):
  at full weight the wrist reached the target exactly (|Lw-tgt| = 0.000, 14 samples).
  So |target - humerus| = |wrist - humerus| <= 0.4994, i.e. inside the RAW reach.
  Whether it was inside 0.4894 (the clamped radius) is NOT established - the humerus
  position was never logged at dock time. That is why the clamp has a NUM2 toggle.

# Bullet spread tiers: only two hands and standing still is dead accurate

> keep bullet spread large when running and firing - the value of walking and aiming at the same time in vanilla game - that is for both one and two hands on the gun. 1 hand on the gun standing still = the first value that comes on after holding RG and starting to aim while still (a middle ground, pretty accurate, room for improvement). 2 hands walking = same as 1 hand walking. Only standing still with 2 hands on the gun = 0 bullet spread. (Tefa 2026-10-10 02:00, work started the same night)

Verbatim record: [`inbox/2026-10-10-re2-zombies-spread-doors-lanes-uninstall.md`](../inbox/2026-10-10-re2-zombies-spread-doors-lanes-uninstall.md)

Four tiers instead of one: **moving** (one or two hands) keeps the game's own loose walk-and-aim
spread; **one hand, standing still** gets the just-raised middle value; **two hands, standing still**
is zero spread. Narrows the older entry just above ("Two-handing is what makes a weapon accurate"):
two hands alone is no longer enough, you also have to stop.

> "this is the right time now to get on other thing working too, holding RG at the back holster to bring out the sub weapon. RG must not be used for "aim" at all anymore as we have a system now for the accuracies, so an Aim and ready state by holding RG is not needed. instead we gotta figure out how to use RG to get to activate the sub weapon from a back holster, needs to be held to have it equipped, releasing it changes it back to the last gun held or no gun, if no gun was equipped. the combo RG + RT pressed while doing the throwing motion with the controller, must throw it in the game"

Verbatim record: [`inbox/2026-10-10c-re2-back-holster-sub-weapon-rg-no-aim.md`](../inbox/2026-10-10c-re2-back-holster-sub-weapon-rg-no-aim.md)

Three parts: the right grip no longer puts the game into its aim state (accuracy is now handled by the spread tiers);
holding the right grip at a spot on the back brings out the sub weapon (knife or grenade) for as long as it is held,
and letting go returns to the gun held before (or empty hands); right grip + trigger with a throwing motion throws it.

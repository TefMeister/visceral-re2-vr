> "ideas for all re games, but starting with re8, hand held maps like Silent hill 2 does. A zoom in, B zoom out, RS to move the map, RT+B floor up, RT+A floor down."

Verbatim record: [`inbox/2026-11-09-re-handheld-map.md`](../inbox/2026-11-09-re-handheld-map.md)

**What it'd take:** show the game's own map screen on a flat panel held in a hand instead of full screen (render the map UI to
a texture and draw it in the world), then route the buttons: A / B to the map's zoom, the right stick to its pan, RT + B / RT + A
to its floor change. Village first, then RE2 and RE3 (all RE Engine, so the panel can be shared). Not checked: whether the map
can be drawn while the game keeps running rather than paused.

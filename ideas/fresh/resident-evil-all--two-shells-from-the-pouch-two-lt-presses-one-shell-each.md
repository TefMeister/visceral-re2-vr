> "Shotgun shells, when coming from the pouch with 2 shells in hand, need 2 presses of LT one after another to insert them."

With two shells in hand from one pouch grab, the first LT press should put in one shell and a second press the other,
rather than one press loading both.

**What it'd take:** the LT insert takes one shell from the hand per press and keeps the second docked at the port.

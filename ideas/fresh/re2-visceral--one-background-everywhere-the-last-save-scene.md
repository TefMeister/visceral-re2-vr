# One background everywhere: the last-save scene

Order: 5010
From: the ideas repo, `games/re2-visceral.md` (<https://github.com/TefMeister/mod-ideas/blob/main/games/re2-visceral.md>), copied 2026-09-27

`[raw]` `[not judged]`

> [re2] change the usual background picture on start up and for all options, extras and options to the one that starts playing after i press STROY. so on launch, there is only the background that shows the last saved location.

In plain words: the moving scene that appears after pressing **Story** (the place you last saved)
should be the only background the menus ever show, from the moment the game starts, including
Options and Extras. The usual start-up picture goes away.

**What it'd take:** finding how the game picks which background the title screen and each menu
shows, and pointing all of them at the last-save one. ⚠️ Unchecked against the game.

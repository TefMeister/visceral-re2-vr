Verbatim record: [`inbox/2026-10-24-re2-six-ideas-polish-cutscenes-stretch-rain-holsters-reload.md`](../inbox/2026-10-24-re2-six-ideas-polish-cutscenes-stretch-rain-holsters-reload.md) (idea 1)

A few polish colours to choose from, clear included, painted on Claire's and Ada's nails. As the game goes on
the polish chips, cracks and fades.

**What it'd take (unchecked):** a nail mask on the hand texture with a colour picked from a short list; the wear
could ride the game's own dirt/blood "Record" system, which already builds up on the hands over a run (see the
Dirty hands HD row on the board).
